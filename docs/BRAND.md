# Brand

Voice, vocabulary, and visual direction for The Code Doctors.
Captured 2026-05-01. Update via PR with founder approval.

## Brand essence

We are doctors for websites. Calm, competent, trustworthy.
We diagnose what's wrong, prescribe the fix, treat it, and provide ongoing care.
We are not an "agency." We are a practice.

The medical metaphor is not a gimmick — it's a positioning moat. Competitors selling
"web maintenance services" sound interchangeable. We sound different from the first word.

## Voice & tone

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Calm, measured, expert | Hyped, exclamatory |
| Speak about outcomes | Speak about features |
| Use medical vocabulary consistently | Mix metaphors |
| Short sentences. Direct. | Run-on marketing copy |
| Explain plainly, like a good GP | Use jargon to sound smart |
| "Your site is sick. We can fix it." | "🚀 Skyrocket your business!" |

### Sample copy

**Yes:**
- "Your website needs a doctor."
- "We diagnose what's broken, prescribe the fix, and keep your site healthy."
- "Book a free checkup. Most sites we examine have at least three things slowly costing them customers."
- "The first checkup is free. We'll send you a 12-page report within 24 hours."

**No:**
- "🚀 Transform your business with our AMAZING web solutions!"
- "We're a passionate team of innovators..."
- "Unlock unprecedented growth with cutting-edge digital strategies."

## Visual direction (proposed — to refine in Phase 1)

### Palette
Dark mode default. Single bold accent color. Generous neutrals.

- **Background**: deep ink (#0A0E13 or similar near-black with a slight blue cast)
- **Surface**: one step lighter (#11161D)
- **Text primary**: near-white (#F2F4F7)
- **Text secondary**: muted (#9AA4B2)
- **Accent**: a calm surgical teal (#3DD9D6) OR a clinical signal red (#F0584C). Pick ONE.
  - Teal = healing, modern, calm. Recommended.
  - Red = urgency, alarm. Reserve for security/alert UI only.
- **Success**: soft green (#4ADE80)
- **Warning**: amber (#F59E0B)

### Typography
- **Display / headlines**: a distinctive but legible sans — Inter Display, General Sans, or PP Neue Montreal
- **Body**: Inter or Geist
- **Mono** (for diagnostic readouts, code, prescriptions): JetBrains Mono or Geist Mono

Headlines are LARGE — 60–96px on desktop hero. Body 16–18px. Generous line-height.

### Imagery
- **No stock photos.** Ever.
- Custom line illustrations (medical-adjacent, abstract, minimal)
- Real Lighthouse screenshots and dashboards
- Real photos of the doctors (when available; until then, abstract avatars)
- 3D / WebGL elements done sparingly and tastefully (heartbeat EKG, abstract pulse)

### Logo direction (to commission/design)
- Wordmark "The Code Doctors" in the display font
- Optional mark: a stethoscope abstracted into a code cursor / chevron
- Monochrome works in white, near-black, and accent color. No gradients.

## Naming for product surfaces

- The free audit tool → **The Free Checkup**
- The full report → **Diagnostic Report** or **Patient File**
- The dashboard → **Patient Portal** (client) / **Practice Dashboard** (staff)
- The status page → **Vitals**

## Tone for system messages
Even error messages stay in voice. Examples:

- ✅ "Checkup complete. We've sent your report to your inbox."
- ✅ "Hmm — something didn't respond. Take a deep breath, and try again in a moment."
- ❌ "Error: Request failed with status 500."
